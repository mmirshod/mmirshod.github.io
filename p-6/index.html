You told me recently that I am still too “young” regarding my emotional intelligence (and I agree with you that), so just imagine how it was two years earlier? I think our relationship wouldn’t last even 2-3 months…

So coming to the previous point, probably it was the best time for both of us to get to know each other specifically in South Korea during the Summer School 2025.
